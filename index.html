FAQ

Straight answers.

I don't have your genetics. Is this for me?

Yes. That's the point of it. You can't change your genetics. The program works on everything you can change.

Is this a pickup course?

No. There are no lines and no tricks. The confidence work is about getting past approach anxiety and having a normal, relaxed conversation.

What exactly do I get?

A 45-minute audit call, a personal 90-day roadmap, and two 1-on-1 calls every week: one on your looks and physique, one on approaching and dating.

Who's on the calls?

Chad. Every call. That's why there are only three spots a month.

How much time does it take each week?

Two calls, plus the work in your roadmap: training, grooming, and getting out to practise.

What results can I expect?

That depends on where you start and how much of the plan you do. Nobody can promise you a specific look or a number of dates, and this page doesn't.

Why only 3 spots?

Each person gets about 25 calls with Chad over the 90 days. Three new people a month is what he can do properly.

What does it cost?

The first call is free. You get the price of the program on that call, before you commit to anything.

What happens to the photos I send?

They're used for your audit and nothing else. They're never posted or shared without your written OK.
